# Sticker Cup — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** [LAUNCH DATE]
**Last Updated:** [LAUNCH DATE]

This Privacy Policy describes how Emre Özmen ("we", "us") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the Sticker Cup mobile application ("App").

## 1. Information We Collect

### Automatically Collected
- **Device information**: device model, OS version, language, country
- **App usage analytics**: gameplay events (quizzes completed, packs opened, cards collected, achievements unlocked) via Google Firebase Analytics
- **Crash reports**: stack traces and device state at the time of a crash, via Google Firebase Crashlytics
- **Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android)**: only if you consent via the App Tracking Transparency prompt, used by Google AdMob to deliver rewarded ads

### Information You Provide
- **Player name**: optional, stored locally on your device
- **In-app purchase records**: processed by Apple App Store / Google Play; we do NOT see your payment details

## 2. How We Use Information
- To operate and improve the game (balance quiz difficulty, card drop rates, etc.)
- To diagnose crashes and bugs
- To deliver rewarded advertising (only with your consent)
- To process in-app purchases

## 3. Third-Party Services
- **Google Firebase** (Analytics, Crashlytics) — [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Google AdMob** — [AdMob Privacy](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)
- **Apple App Store / Google Play Billing** — payment processing

## 4. Children's Privacy
Sticker Cup is rated for ages 4+. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. The App does not include social features, chat, or user-generated content.

## 5. Your Rights
- **Opt out of analytics**: Settings → Notifications & Tracking → toggle off
- **Opt out of personalized ads**: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Sticker Cup
- **Delete your data**: Settings → Account → Reset Save (clears all local data)
- **EU/UK users**: Right to access, correction, deletion, and portability under GDPR. Contact us below.

## 6. Data Retention
- Local save data: stored on your device until you uninstall or reset
- Analytics events: retained by Firebase for up to 14 months
- Crash reports: retained by Firebase for up to 90 days

## 7. Data Security
We use industry-standard practices to protect data in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. No method is 100% secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in-app or via the App Store listing.
